# DROPi Delivery — Publisher Application Profile

Use this file as the canonical wording source when applying to affiliate/publisher networks. Keep every answer truthful to the live site and current operating stage. Do not invent traffic, followers, revenue, conversions, mailing-list size or approval status.

Verified against the repository: 2026-09-16.

## Core identity

**Publisher / website name:** DROPi Delivery

**Business model:** Independent editorial commerce / affiliate publication. DROPi Delivery publishes original practical guides for delivery drivers, couriers and small ecommerce operators. It does not manufacture, stock or fulfil the products and software covered by the site.

**Primary audience:**
- delivery drivers and couriers;
- bicycle and e-bike couriers;
- food-delivery workers;
- owner-drivers and small van operators;
- small ecommerce sellers who need shipping, packaging and dispatch tools.

**Initial geographic focus:** Ireland and Europe.

**Site language:** English.

**Current editorial library:** 52 original guides across five categories: Driver Essentials, Vehicle & Van, Bike / E-bike, Food Delivery and Shipping Tools.

## Reusable short description

DROPi Delivery is an independent English-language editorial commerce site for delivery drivers, couriers and small ecommerce sellers in Ireland and Europe. We publish original workflow-first buying guides covering delivery equipment, vehicle organisation, bike/e-bike gear, food-delivery equipment and ecommerce shipping tools. The site does not hold stock or operate a checkout; monetisation is through clearly disclosed affiliate referrals to relevant third-party retailers and software providers.

## Reusable longer description

DROPi Delivery helps people involved in delivery work and small-scale ecommerce choose equipment and software around real operational problems rather than around generic product catalogues. The publication covers driver essentials, vehicle and van organisation, bike/e-bike delivery, food-delivery equipment and ecommerce shipping/packaging workflows. Content is original and written for an Ireland/Europe audience, with commercial recommendations placed contextually inside relevant guides. DROPi Delivery does not manufacture, stock, sell or fulfil the products described on the site. Where an approved affiliate relationship exists, commercial links are disclosed and routed through the relevant partner/network tracking supplied to DROPi.

## Editorial standards to describe if asked

- Content is written around distinct user/workflow problems rather than thin keyword variants.
- DROPi does not claim physical product testing unless that testing has actually been performed and documented.
- Prices are not presented as permanently current where no reliable refresh process exists.
- Product specifications and programme terms can change, so readers are directed to confirm important details with the provider.
- Affiliate relationships are disclosed.
- DROPi does not present itself as the seller of third-party products.

## Application checklist

Before submitting any new publisher/network application:

1. Confirm the live site is reachable.
2. Confirm the final application URL matches `SITE_URL` where possible.
3. Confirm `/privacy`, `/cookies`, `/terms`, `/affiliate-disclosure` and `/contact` render correctly.
4. Confirm `CONTACT_EMAIL` is a real public business/editorial address.
5. Use only measured traffic/performance figures.
6. Use this document for wording, but re-check the network's current programme terms.
7. Do not claim advertiser approval before the network shows it.
8. Keep tax, payout and account credentials out of GitHub.
9. Save programme-specific approval/tracking evidence before changing the repository status to ACTIVE.
