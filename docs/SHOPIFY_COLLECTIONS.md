# DROPi Delivery — Shopify Collection Map

Established: 2026-09-16

The connected Shopify store uses smart collections so products are routed automatically by tags.

| Collection | Tag |
|---|---|
| Delivery Driver Essentials | `driver` |
| Bike & E-bike Courier | `bike-courier` |
| Food Delivery Gear | `food-delivery` |
| Power & Charging | `power-charging` |
| Vehicle & Van Essentials | `vehicle-van` |
| Dispatch & Packaging | `dispatch-packaging` |

## Product activation rule

Products may be staged as DRAFT while sourcing is being verified.

A product must not become ACTIVE until:

1. supplier identity is known;
2. fulfilment to Ireland is available;
3. supplier product cost is known;
4. shipping/direct fulfilment cost is known;
5. customs/import cost is included where applicable;
6. payment/Shopify direct cost is accounted for;
7. expected unit profit is at least EUR 2.00;
8. product description and delivery claims are truthful;
9. no fabricated reviews, stock, discounts or delivery promises are present.

## Tagging rule

Use every relevant collection tag, not only one. Example: a 20,000 mAh power bank intended for bike couriers may use both `bike-courier` and `power-charging`.
