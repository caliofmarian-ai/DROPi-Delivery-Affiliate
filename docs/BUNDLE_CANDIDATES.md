# DROPi Delivery — Bundle Candidates

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Purpose: evaluate bundles only when combining products can improve customer value and contribution while keeping fulfilment operationally truthful.

## Rule

A bundle is not `APPROVED` merely because its paper margin is positive. It must pass the same evidence gates as an individual SKU:

- exact supplier/account price;
- exact shipping to Ireland;
- evidence that the products can ship together as assumed;
- packaging cost;
- payment/Shopify cost;
- customs/import cost where applicable;
- returns/refunds workflow;
- product compliance;
- expected contribution >= EUR 2.00.

## BUNDLE-001 — Courier Bike Security & Visibility Kit

Proposed components:

1. AXA DWN 30 USB-C Bike Light Set
2. Kryptonite Keeper 510 Folding Lock 100cm

Supplier path: Tormino, Netherlands.

### Current public screening inputs

- AXA public Tormino price: EUR 25.99 incl. VAT.
- Kryptonite public Tormino price: EUR 64.99 incl. VAT.
- Public entry dropship discount referenced by Tormino: 5%.
- Ireland standard parcel shipping published by Tormino: EUR 9.95.
- Neutral packaging: EUR 0.50 per item.
- Test bundle selling price: EUR 124.90.
- Conservative Shopify/payment screening fee used by the project includes the standard online-card percentage/fixed component and Irish VAT on that processing fee.

Using those public assumptions, the screening contribution is about **EUR 24 per completed bundle order** before fixed monthly operating costs.

This is **not final profit** and is not approval evidence. It assumes one Ireland parcel charge covers both products, which has not yet been verified at account checkout.

### Why this bundle is useful

- both products target the same bike/e-bike courier;
- both come from one EU supplier path;
- both are practical, durable gear rather than novelty products;
- the bundle can reduce the impact of fixed per-order shipping compared with selling a cheap accessory alone;
- the proposed total stays below the combined current public single-item benchmarks used during screening.

### Status

`BUNDLE_CANDIDATE / HOLD FOR ACCOUNT-LEVEL SHIPPING EVIDENCE`

Do not create or publish the bundle in Shopify until Tormino confirms:

1. one-parcel shipping behaviour for the two products;
2. actual dropship account pricing;
3. exact Ireland checkout cost;
4. neutral packaging charges;
5. returns/refunds path.
